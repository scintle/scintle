---
title: Questions it asks
description: When Scintle asks instead of guessing, and what each answer does.
---

Some calls are too important to guess. Then Scintle asks one question in a banner at the top of the board, at most one per session. **Later** is always fine; the question waits.

## "Did these merge?"

Scintle never merges two ideas on its own. When a session sounds like two ideas became one, it asks. **Yes** creates the merged idea, linked to both parents. **No** keeps them separate.

## "A new idea, or the same one narrowed?"

When an idea changes shape and Scintle isn't sure whether it's a different idea (a pivot, with a new ID) or the same one with a smaller scope (a narrowing, same ID), it records its best guess and asks you to confirm.

## "Are you working on this again?"

When a session picks up an idea that was parked or dropped, Scintle asks. **Yes** brings it back (resumed or revived); **No** leaves it as it was. One answer settles the question for that idea.

The quote in a question is credited to whoever said it: you, Claude, or a document.
