---
title: Correcting the board
description: Keep, remove or change what Scintle inferred, move ideas between stages, and record why an idea ended.
---

Scintle infers; you correct. Nothing it infers is final, and every correction can be undone.

## Inferred events

An inferred event has a dotted outline. In an idea's timeline, each one shows the words it came from and who said them: you, Claude, or a document. Then:

- **Keep**: it's right. The outline turns solid.
- **Not this**: it didn't happen. The event leaves the timeline, and the idea's stage and title are worked out again without it.
- **Change type**: it happened, but it was a narrowing, not a pivot (for example).

A removed event can be brought back with **Restore**. After any change, the toast offers **Undo**.

## Moving an idea

The stage menu on a card or in the panel moves an idea along: mark it planned or released, park it, resume it, revive it.

**Drop** asks why:

- **Not interested**: you looked at it and never wanted it. Only you can set this; Scintle never infers it.
- **Crowded market**, **No real demand**, **Not feasible**, or **Other**.

The reason shows as a pill on the card and can be used as a filter. Reviving an idea clears it.

## Adding an idea by hand

Press `n` or use **New idea**. A hand-made idea is just as real as an inferred one: later sessions that talk about it attach to it.
