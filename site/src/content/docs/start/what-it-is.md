---
title: What Scintle is
description: Why ideas get lost across Claude Code chats, and how Scintle keeps them.
---

Ideas outlive the chats they start in. You sketch something in one session, narrow it in another, rename it in a third, and drop it for a reason you'll have forgotten by spring. Scrolling old chats doesn't bring the story back.

Scintle is a Claude Code plugin that keeps that story for you. It reads your sessions as you work and keeps a board of your ideas. Each idea has:

- **A stable ID** (`I-007`) that survives renames and narrowing, so "the ledger idea" and "Scintle" stay one card.
- **A typed history**: born, renamed, narrowed, pivoted, spawned, merged, planned, parked, released, dropped. Every event carries the words it came from and a link back to the session.
- **Lineage**: when an idea turns into a different one, the new idea gets its own ID, linked to where it came from.

## What makes it different

- **It finds your ideas on its own.** You never type an idea in or fill in a form. Scintle spots ideas in the chats you already had and the ones you're having now, infers events and shows them as inferred (dotted) until you keep or correct them.
- **You correct, you don't approve.** When it's wrong, one click fixes it; when it's unsure, it asks one question at a time, at most one per session.
- **It stays on your machine.** It reads Claude's own folder, stores a local database, and reads sessions with your own `claude` login. See [What it reads, stores and sends](/scintle/trust/privacy/).

## Who it's for

People who use Claude Code to think, not just to code: builders who brainstorm, research and plan in sessions, and want to know where each idea went.
